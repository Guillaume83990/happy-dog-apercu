Saint-Tropez

My village, and where Happy Dog began. The harbour, the narrow streets, the café terraces, the Place des Lices on market days: here a dog meets crowds, noise, other dogs and scooters. We start at your home, then move to these everyday places, so your dog stays calm there, even in high summer.

Dog behaviourist in Saint-Tropez

Gassin

The hilltop village, its vineyards and country lanes. Many dogs here live with a garden: we work on recall, on visitors, and on what happens when the gate opens, to avoid running off at the first gap in the hedge.

Training and retraining

Ramatuelle

Pampelonne, the villas, the vineyards and Cap Camarat. In summer many families settle here for the season: educational walks, dog sitting in your home while you are out, and a dog that keeps its bearings in a house it knows less well.

Walks and home dog sitting

Grimaud

The old village and Port Grimaud, with its canals, bridges and boats. Quaysides, pontoons, getting on board: a dog that is relaxed by the water can be taught, just like loose-lead walking among the crowds.

Loose-lead walking and recall

Cogolin

A lively town, with its markets, its shopping streets and Port Cogolin. For a puppy discovering town life, it is the ideal setting: pavements, noise, terraces, other dogs, all discovered at its own pace.

Puppy training

Sainte-Maxime

Facing Saint-Tropez across the Gulf: the seafront promenade, the beaches, the busy town centre. Here we work on calm among passers-by, and on a recall that holds when everything catches your dog’s attention.

Reactivity, fears, barking

La Croix-Valmer

Between vineyards, hills and Gigaro beach, a town close to nature. Walks here are beautiful, and this is where a solid recall and relaxed lead walking make all the difference.

Educational walks

Cavalaire-sur-Mer

The long beach, the marina, a town that comes alive in summer. Holidaymakers or year-round residents, I help your dog enjoy the season without stress, for your dog or for you.

Dog sitting during your stay

Le Rayol-Canadel-sur-Mer

The Corniche des Maures, between forest and sea. Magnificent nature, paths that climb and drop: we prepare your dog for outings in the wild, safely.

Recall and outings

La Môle

A small village in the heart of the Maures, surrounded by forest and vineyards. Training here often happens in the open air: recall, the environment, meeting other animals.

The right start

Le Plan-de-la-Tour

A quiet inland village among hills and vineyards. Family dogs, often with plenty of land: we work on the basics, welcoming visitors and life with children.

Puppies and family life

La Garde-Freinet

The hilltop village of the Maures, with its cork-oak forests and trails. For dogs living far from the bustle, I come to you, as everywhere on the Gulf, and by video call between sessions if you wish.

Online coaching

Frequently asked questions

Where I work: your questions

Which towns do you cover?

The 12 towns of the Gulf of Saint-Tropez: Saint-Tropez, Gassin, Ramatuelle, Grimaud, Cogolin, Sainte-Maxime, La Croix-Valmer, Cavalaire-sur-Mer, Le Rayol-Canadel-sur-Mer, La Môle, Le Plan-de-la-Tour and La Garde-Freinet.

Do you come to my home?

Yes: at your home first, where your dog lives, then in your everyday places: beach, town, harbour, trails, sometimes with another dog.

Do you work outside the Gulf of Saint-Tropez?

Elsewhere in the Var on request, and anywhere in the world by video call.

I am on holiday on the Gulf: can you come?

Yes: educational walks, dog sitting in your home and sessions during your stay, in every town of the Gulf.

What are your prices?

There is no single price: it depends on the service, the number of sessions and the travel to your town. Tell me your town and your situation: after a first conversation, I send you a clear proposal, with its price, before any session. You pay me directly, with no commitment.
