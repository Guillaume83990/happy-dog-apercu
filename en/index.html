My approach

Here, we are not looking for perfect dogs or perfect owners. We are trying to understand our dogs better, and through them, ourselves.

The dog

Its genetics, its breed, its history, what it has learned, its living environment, its behaviour.

About me

Vanessa Vanlerberghe

“I am not a dog trainer, but a certified dog educator.”

I have been coming to Saint-Tropez since the 1980s and have lived here all year round since 2011. I had many careers, from long-haul flight attendant to luxury hospitality, until the day I understood that wherever my dog was, I was where I belonged.

Today a dog behaviourist and founder of Happy Dog Saint-Tropez, I observe, analyse and pass on what happens in the unique relationship between a dog and its humans, where each one grows through the other, like a mirror. Samba led me here; Bahia, the apple of my eye, is by my side every day.
