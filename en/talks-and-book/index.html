The book by Vanessa Vanlerberghe

Nos chiens, ces héros

“A dog never comes into a life, into a family, by chance. Never.”

These stories, first jotted down in a notebook, were born from my sessions as a dog behaviourist and trainer on the Gulf of Saint-Tropez. Every day I observe, analyse and pass on what happens between a dog and its human: a unique relationship, in which each grows through the other, like a mirror.

In these stories, the dog becomes

  1. A mirror

    It brings our fears, the things left unsaid and our impatience into the light.

  2. A support

    It teaches us to slow down and come back to the present moment: a game, a cuddle, a walk in the forest.

  3. A catalyst for change

    It reveals buried emotions and old wounds, and often becomes the beginning of a transformation.

This collection is also a tribute to Samba and Bahia, my two Australian Shepherds, who guide me in my personal as well as my professional life, and to all the wonderful people that dogs have brought my way.

Frequently asked questions

The book and the talks: your questions

What is the book Nos chiens, ces héros about?

It is a collection of illustrated short stories (184 pages), born from my sessions as a dog behaviourist. Each story follows a dog and its humans: the dog acts in turn as a mirror, a support or a catalyst for change.

Where do the stories come from?

From my work in the field: I first wrote them down in a notebook after sometimes demanding sessions, then turned them into short stories. The book is also a tribute to Samba and Bahia, my two Australian Shepherds, who support me and help me grow in my work every day.

Is the book available in English?

The book is written in French. Four of its stories are on video on this page, also in French.

Where can I buy the book?

In bookshops around the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, at newsagents, at the tourist office, and online on Amazon. For a signed copy, contact me: I can send it to you in France or abroad.

Who are your talks for?

Schools and children, families, companies, private clubs, associations, partners and specialist shops wishing to host an event. Each talk is built for its audience, and is always rich in conversations and encounters.

How can I invite you to give a talk?

Message me on WhatsApp or call +33 6 20 70 88 73: tell me who and where you are, your audience and the date you have in mind, and I will reply personally.
