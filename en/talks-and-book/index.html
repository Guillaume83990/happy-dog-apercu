The book

Nos chiens, ces héros

“A dog never comes into a life, into a family, by chance. Never.”

A dog always helps us move forward and see what surrounds us differently. Sometimes it teaches us to slow down and come back to the present moment, for a game, a cuddle, a walk in nature.

But it can also reveal our fears, the things left unsaid, our impatience, our old wounds. It then becomes a revealer, and often the beginning of a transformation.

This collection is a tribute to Samba and Bahia, my two Australian Shepherds, and to all the people that dogs have brought my way.
