Frequently asked questions

Online dog coaching: your questions

Does an online session really work with a dog?

Yes, for many questions: understanding a behaviour, what is happening between you and your dog, setting guidance and exercises for your daily life. If a situation needs your dog to be observed in person, I will tell you honestly.

Do I need to live on the Gulf of Saint-Tropez?

No: by video call, I work with owners in France and abroad. On the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, I also come to your home.

How is it different from a home session?

At home, I meet your dog in its own environment and we work in real situations. Online, we work on understanding, the relationship and everyday guidance, wherever you are.

How do I get in touch?

On WhatsApp, by message or voice note: your dog, your question and your country. I will reply personally.

Which dogs do you work with?

Puppies and adult dogs of all breeds: every dog has its own story, and so does every relationship.

I come to Saint-Tropez in summer: can we also meet in person?

Yes: during your stay, I come to your home on the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, and we carry on by video call for the rest of the year.

What are your prices?

There is no single price, because every situation is different. By video call there is no travel: the price depends on what your dog needs and the number of sessions. Tell me about your situation: after a first conversation, I send you a clear proposal, with its price, before any session. You pay me directly, with no commitment.
