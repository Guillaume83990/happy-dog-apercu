Who is responsible for your data

Vanessa Vanlerberghe, Happy Dog Saint-Tropez (sole trader), 83990 Saint-Tropez, France. For any question about your data: happydog.sttropez@gmail.com or +33 6 20 70 88 73.

What is collected, and why

The “And you?” window (question, giveaway, special offer)

When you answer it, Vanessa receives: your answer, your first name, your dog’s name, age and breed, your phone number, your Instagram account if you give it for a giveaway, and the boxes you ticked.

  • To answer you and contact you again, if you ticked the box for it: this is your consent.
  • To run a giveaway: record your entry, draw the winner and let them know, as set out in the rules.
  • To send you news (tips, next giveaways), only if you ticked the optional box.

Your messages

The messages, calls and emails you send Vanessa are only used to answer you and to prepare your sessions.

No data is sold, rented or exchanged. No automated decision is made from your answers.

Who can see it

Vanessa only. To work, the website relies on technical services, which do not use your data for their own purposes:

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited, Dublin): your answers to the “And you?” window are stored in Vanessa’s private Google spreadsheet, and she receives an alert in her Gmail inbox.
  • OVH (Roubaix, France): website hosting.
  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited): only if you choose to message Vanessa on WhatsApp. Your message then follows WhatsApp’s own rules.

Google and WhatsApp may process some data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Keep only if Vanessa shares her spreadsheet for maintenance: Sud Web Project, which maintains the website, may access it occasionally for technical work, without ever using your data.

How long

  • Your answers and contact details: 3 years after your last exchange with Vanessa, then erased.
  • Giveaway entry, without consent to receive news: 6 months after the end of the giveaway (to be able to prove the draw), then erased.

Your rights

At any time, you can ask to see your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its use, object to its use, get a copy of it, or withdraw your consent. Just write to happydog.sttropez@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +33 6 20 70 88 73. Vanessa replies within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the French data protection authority (CNIL): cnil.fr, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no tracking tools. That is why no banner asks for your consent.

Only one small technical memory is used in your browser: during your visit, it remembers that you have already seen the website’s introduction, so it is not shown again. It is erased when you close the tab and is used for nothing else.

The Instagram, Facebook, Google, WhatsApp and Amazon buttons are simple links: nothing is loaded from these websites unless you click.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), with strict security rules. Forms are checked before sending, and the answers spreadsheet can only be opened with Vanessa’s account.
