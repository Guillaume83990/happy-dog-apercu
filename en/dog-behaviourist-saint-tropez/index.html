My method

« The best way to understand your dog: meet it, watch it and talk it through with you. »

Behaviour never happens by chance. Before trying to change it, I look for what it expresses: in your dog, in you, and between you.

Trained in psychology, I look at the human as much as at the dog. Because the dog is often a mirror: it reveals our fears, our impatience, our tensions. Working on the relationship is often what changes everything.

  • The dogIts breed, its history, its surroundings: what triggers its behaviour.
  • The humanThe way you live and communicate with it, without judgement.
  • The relationshipA tailored programme, with a positive method, to rebuild trust.

“The dog becomes a revealer: of buried emotions, old wounds, impulses towards healing.”

Frequently asked questions

Before you call me

My dog is reactive with other dogs: what should I do?

Don't stay alone with it: the earlier we step in, the simpler it is. We start by watching your dog at home and in real situations to understand what triggers its reaction, then we build a tailored programme, with a positive method.

My dog barks when I leave: is it separation anxiety?

Perhaps, but not necessarily: boredom, fear or a habit can show the same signs. Watching your dog at home is how we understand what is going on, before deciding what to change.

What's the difference between a dog trainer and a behaviourist?

A trainer teaches the dog cues; a behaviourist looks for why it behaves as it does, to change the behaviour at its source. I do both, and I also work with you: the relationship matters as much as the dog.

My dog has bitten: can you help?

Yes, aggression towards dogs or people is one of the situations I work with. Just one thing to do: call me on +33 6 20 70 88 73. If your dog has bitten a person, French law also requires a declaration to the town hall and a behavioural assessment by a vet: I support you alongside.

How many sessions does it take?

Every dog is different: we talk about it after the assessment, depending on your dog and your goals. And if your dog no longer needs sessions, I'll tell you.

Do you come to homes across the Gulf?

Yes, in the 12 towns of the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, elsewhere in the Var on request, and anywhere in the world by video call.

What are your prices?

Every dog is different: tell me about your situation and I will reply personally.
