First cues

Six cues to train your puppy

Simple cues, learnt through play with positive puppy training, that will serve your dog for life. And you learn at the same time.

01

Sit - down

The first two cues, the basis for all the others.

02

Drop it - put it down

Letting go of what is in its mouth, as a fair trade, without conflict.

03

Loose-lead walking

Walking by your side without pulling, from the very first outings.

04

Recall

Coming back when you call, alone and then with other dogs.

05

Stay

Waiting calmly, at the right moment, where you ask.

06

Socialisation

Situations, people, other dogs: at ease everywhere, with everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Puppy training: your questions

At what age should puppy training start?

As soon as your puppy arrives. The first weeks matter a lot: that's when it discovers its home, its family and the world outside. The earlier the basics are in place, the easier everything becomes.

How many sessions does a puppy need?

Every puppy is different: we talk about it at our first meeting, depending on its character and your goals. And if it no longer needs sessions, I'll tell you.

Can the whole family take part?

Yes, and it is even best: your puppy learns with the people it lives with. The videos I send you on WhatsApp let everyone go over the cues again.

Where do the sessions take place?

At your home first, then in your everyday places: pavements, beach, town, harbour, sometimes with another dog. In the 12 towns of the Gulf of Saint-Tropez (Saint-Tropez, Gassin, Ramatuelle, Grimaud and Port Grimaud, Cogolin, Sainte-Maxime, La Croix-Valmer…), elsewhere in the Var on request, and anywhere in the world by video call.

My dog isn't a puppy any more: is it too late?

No, an adult dog can learn everything, at its own pace. That is what training and retraining is for. Training and retraining

What are your prices?

There is no single price, because every puppy and every family is different. The price depends on your puppy's age, what you want it to learn first, the number of sessions, the place (at home, outdoors or by video call) and the travel to your town. Tell me about your situation: after a first conversation, I send you a clear proposal, with its price, before any session. You pay me directly, with no commitment.
