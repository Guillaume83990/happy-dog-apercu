My dogs

Samba and Bahia, my two Australian Shepherds.

Over the years I have shared my life with a German Shepherd, a Weimaraner and two wonderful Australian Shepherds.

Samba

Rescued from a breeder where she had been mistreated, she left suddenly in 2019, barely 4 years old. The vet explained that she was holding on only out of love, and for fear of leaving me alone. She is the one who led me here.

Bahia

The same little model, a different coat. Bahia arrived just after, and she is the apple of my eye. She is with me every day, in sessions, on walks and at talks.

My approach

« I am not a dog trainer in the old sense, but a certified educator. »

If you want to take your best friend everywhere, it is essential that your dog is well trained and knows how to behave, in every situation and every setting. Training does not stop when the puppy arrives: it goes on at any age.

I use positive methods based on reward and on your dog's well-being within the family, with programmes tailored to its age, breed, character and your family life.

  • The dogIts physical and psychological needs, its history, what it is expressing.
  • The humanThe way you communicate with your dog: eye contact, gestures, posture.
  • The relationshipComplete trust between you and your dog, built with kindness.

And I still have a dream: to bring Bahia, and maybe your dogs too, into schools and care homes.

Frequently asked questions

Vanessa Vanlerberghe: your questions

Who is Vanessa Vanlerberghe?

A certified dog trainer and behaviourist, a psychology graduate, the founder of Happy Dog Saint-Tropez and the author of the book Nos chiens, ces héros. I work with dogs and their humans across the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, and by video call everywhere else.

Are you qualified?

Yes: a certified dog trainer and behaviourist, trained with leading dog trainers in France and, remotely, in the United States.

How long have you been working with dogs?

I set up as a dog trainer and behaviourist in January 2023, in Saint-Tropez, where I have lived all year round since 2011.

What is your method?

Positive training based on reward and the dog's well-being, with programmes tailored to its age, breed, character and family setting.

Why does psychology matter?

Because a relationship has two sides. Trained in non-verbal communication, I look at the human as much as the dog: very often, the dog is a mirror.
