Frequently asked questions

Sitting and walks: your questions

Does the care take place in my home?

Yes, always in your home, never in kennels: your dog keeps its house, its bearings and its habits.

Are the walks one-to-one or in a group?

Both: alone to work on a specific cue, or in a group to burn off energy, learn to stay calm and work on its self-control with other dogs.

My dog has never been looked after: will it cope?

We get to know each other before you leave, so it knows me and feels confident. And you follow its stay on video.

Will I hear from you while I’m away?

Yes, videos on WhatsApp to reassure you, and I can be reached by message or voice note.

Which towns do you cover?

Saint-Tropez, Gassin and Ramatuelle.

What are your prices?

There is no single price, because every dog and every stay is different. The price depends on the service (walk, visit or dog-sitting at your home), its length, your dates and the travel to your town. Send me your dates and your situation: I reply with a clear proposal, with its price, before anything is confirmed. You pay me directly, with no commitment.
