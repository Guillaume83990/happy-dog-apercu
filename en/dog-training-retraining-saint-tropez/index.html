Sound familiar?

Lead, recall, basics: what owners often tell me.

  1. 01

    “He never really learnt the basics”

    Sit, down, stay, recall: an adult dog can learn everything, at its own pace.

  2. 02

    “She pulls on the lead and won't come back”

    Loose-lead walking, stop, recall, then off-lead walking, in town as well as out in nature.

  3. 03

    “He has picked up bad habits”

    Excessive barking, stealing, jumping up, house-soiling: we take things back, without rushing.

  4. 04

    “We have just adopted her”

    Shelter, pound, a dog from far away: sometimes you have to start from scratch, with patience.

  5. 05

    “I want him to be fine everywhere”

    Pavements, café terraces, the beach, other dogs: cues that hold, even with distractions all around.

Fears, reactivity, aggression, anxiety when you leave? That is the work of a dog behaviourist, and it has its own page: Behaviour problems.

Frequently asked questions

Dog training: your questions

Can my adult dog still learn?

Yes. I work with puppies as well as adult dogs of all breeds. An adult dog learns at its own pace, with a positive method and a programme tailored to its age, breed, character and your family life.

How many sessions does it take?

Every dog is different: we talk about it at our first meeting, depending on your dog and your goals. And if your dog no longer needs sessions, I'll tell you.

Where do the sessions take place?

At your home first, where your dog lives, then in your everyday places: beach, town, harbour, sometimes with another dog. In the 12 towns of the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, from Sainte-Maxime to Cavalaire-sur-Mer via Saint-Tropez, Grimaud, Cogolin, Gassin and Ramatuelle, elsewhere in the Var on request, and anywhere in the world by video call.

What method do you use?

Positive dog training, with tailored programmes, one-to-one or in a group. I'm not a dog “trainer” in the old sense but a certified educator: your dog learns, and you learn with it.

What happens between sessions?

You receive my feedback on WhatsApp, with the videos of the session, so you can go over each cue at your own pace. And I can be reached 7 days a week, by message or voice note.

What are your prices?

There is no single price, because every programme is built around your dog. The price depends on what your dog needs, its age, the number of sessions, the place (at home, outdoors or by video call) and the travel to your town. Tell me about your situation: after a first conversation, I send you a clear proposal, with its price, before any session. You pay me directly, with no commitment.
